# Bidding on construction tenders in German SME contractors: practitioner reality and pain points

Reading guide. Every finding carries a label for the kind of evidence:
**[STAT]** survey / official statistic / measured case study, **[PRACT]** statement by a practitioner (quoted in a study or press),
**[LAW]** lawyer or court, **[ACAD]** thesis / textbook, **[JOBAD]** job advertisement, **[VENDOR]** vendor claim (weak evidence).
Non-German sources are marked **(AT)** / **(CH)**. Inferences are only in the "Inferences" blocks. Research date: 2026-11-21.

Best single source found: IfM Bonn, IfM-Materialien 307 (Oct/Nov 2024) — three time-measured case studies with verbatim interviews.
Its limits: n = 3, all **Elektrotechnik** (building trade, not Tiefbau), NRW, municipal public tenders only. The press reported it as "three construction firms".

## 1. Finding and selecting tenders; bid/no-bid in practice

### Takeaway
Small firms mostly do not search: they bid when a Vergabestelle or the local grapevine points them to a tender, and stay in their own and neighbouring municipalities; mid-sized firms search across several portals and find that search itself costs 12–22 % of their per-bid effort. The bid/no-bid decision is described as a quick fit check (portfolio, Eignung, time, staff availability, award criteria) costing only 1–7 % of bid effort — no source found a formal scoring in SMEs.

### Cited Findings
- **[STAT/PRACT]** Company A (8 employees): the owner alone handles bids, bids 2–5 times a year, "bewirbt sich ausschließlich auf öffentliche Ausschreibungen, auf die er seitens der Vergabestellen hingewiesen wird oder von denen er in der Region frühzeitig etwas erfährt. Eine explizite Suche nach öffentlichen Ausschreibungen führt er nicht durch." Focus: own and neighbouring municipalities. Motives are partly social pressure from the municipality to take part. — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 18](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT/PRACT]** Company B (36 employees, 15–20 public bids/year): bids mainly "Leerlaufzeiten zwischen Bauprojekten zu minimieren" (i.e. capacity/Auslastung-driven) and for prestige projects; restricted to the region "weil die Mitarbeiterinnen und Mitarbeiter nicht bereit sind, für überregionale Baustellen tageweise ihre Familien zu verlassen." Three people involved: Meister, commercial lead, a clerk. — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 21](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT/PRACT]** Company C (70 employees, 36–48 public bids/year, municipal + Land + Bund): sees public tenders as attractive "weil sich oftmals wenige Bieter beteiligen und daher die Erfolgschancen hoch sind". Search costs 12–22 % of the cost per submitted bid; for B 3–5 %; for A zero. — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 24–25](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** Pre-check (Vorabprüfung) = fit with own portfolio, Eignungskriterien, "zeitliche Machbarkeit, die ausreichende Verfügbarkeit personeller Ressourcen", possible Bietergemeinschaft, which award criteria decide. Share of per-bid cost: A ca. 3 %, B ca. 1 %, C 5–7 %. — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 10–11, 18–25](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** Why private aggregator portals exist: below the EU threshold the publication medium for construction is not fixed (daily papers, official gazettes, any internet portal, § 12 VOB/A); "öffentlichen Plattformen als sehr bedienerunfreundlich gelten"; when a Vergabestelle publishes only on its own site, firms "immer mehrere Plattformen im Blick halten müssen". — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 9–10, 30](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[PRACT]** Portal fragmentation, Company B: "Die Unterlagen einholen wird bei einem Tool gemacht, die Unterlagen abgeben wird bei dem anderen Tool gemacht. [XY] macht das z. B. mit einer ganz anderen Software." and "Ich mach mir da so Zettelchen, wo man klicken muss, um an die Unterlagen ranzukommen. […] Also ohne meine Zettel würde ich mich da jetzt nicht zurechtfinden." — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 23](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** Only 57 % of Berlin and 63 % of Brandenburg construction firms were bidding on public tenders at all (Fachgemeinschaft Bau spring survey 2018; ca. a quarter of members answered; n not stated). Named reasons: bureaucracy and "nicht auskömmliche Preise"; Nachweise cost "mehrere Tage für einen Kalkulator". — [Vergabeblog, 24.07.2018](https://www.vergabeblog.de/2018-07-24/umfrageergebnis-fg-bau-oeffentliche-auftraege-wenig-attraktiv/)
- **[PRACT]** Stefan Zirngibl, Schlossermeister, 5 employees, Kreishandwerksmeister Weilheim: "Für zweimal elf Meter Geländer soll ich ein Leistungsverzeichnis von 40 Seiten durchlesen und bearbeiten" … "Ein großer Betrieb kann das leisten, ein kleiner nicht." … "Da arbeite ich doch lieber fünf Stunden in der Werkstatt". A comparable private offer: 3–4 pages, 1.5 hours. — [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung, 22.10.2018](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=93714)
- **[PRACT]** Joachim Krimmer, president HWK Ulm: one school-kitchen tender = a 600-page, 1.5 kg folder; "Für 10.000 Euro Umsatz arbeitet ein Handwerker in diesem Fall rund 150 Stunden", of which 58 hours pure bureaucracy; craftsmen avoid jobs "wenn mehr als ein Drittel unproduktive Zeiten sind". — [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung, 22.10.2018](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=93714)
- **[ACAD] (AT, 2009, textbook-based)** Go/no-go criteria named by Girmscheid (2006): Kundenbeziehung, Konkurrenzsituation, eigene Leistungsfähigkeit, eigene Auslastung/Ressourcenverfügbarkeit, eigene Wertschöpfung, Aufwand für die Angebotsbearbeitung, Risiken, Ertragspotenzial; plus strategic reasons (new market, reference project, publicity). — [Selberherr, TU Wien 2009, pp. 24–25](https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/13599/2/Selberherr%20Julia%20-%202009%20-%20Angebotsstrategien%20unter%20Beruecksichtigung%20von...pdf)
- **[STAT]** Market frame: 199,334 reported public awards in 2024; construction ca. 48 % of awards by number; ca. 88 % below EU thresholds; SMEs won 82 % of awards (163,215; partly a definitional change); price was the sole award criterion in ca. 63 % of awards. — [cosinex blog on the BMWE Vergabestatistik 2024](https://blog.cosinex.de/2026/08/27/vergabestatistik-bericht-2024/)
- **[VENDOR, self-declared AI-written page, no source given]** "Ein mittelständisches Bauunternehmen bewirbt sich im Jahr auf 30 bis 60 Ausschreibungen." — [ki-syndikat.de](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/usecases/bauwesen/06-ausschreibung/) (consistent in magnitude with IfM Company C's 36–48, but unsourced)

### Inferences
- A step **before "Check"** exists in every source: finding the tender and a short fit decision (IfM steps 1 and 2). A board that starts at "Check" starts after the first decision practitioners make. For small firms the entry is "we were told about it / invited"; for mid-sized firms it is multi-portal search.
- The real bid/no-bid drivers named by practitioners are capacity gaps (B), region/travel willingness of crews (A, B), expected number of competitors (C), relationship to the Vergabestelle (A), and sheer paperwork relative to job size (Zirngibl). These match the textbook list except that "risk in the documents" is not something the interviewed SMEs name as a selection criterion — risk is noticed later, while calculating.
- The fit check is cheap in hours (1–7 %) but it gates 50–90 % of spend; a wrong "go" is paid for in the calculation step.

### Gaps
- No German figure found for **tenders screened per bid submitted**. No survey found on how formal the bid/no-bid decision is in SMEs (scoring vs. owner's gut feeling); the "gut feeling of the owner" picture is supported only by the IfM case A (owner decides alone) and is otherwise my reading.
- Nothing found specifically on Submissionsanzeiger use, on invitations to Beschränkte Ausschreibung (share of bids that come from invitations), or on private invitations from architects/GUs. The Vergabestatistik breakdown by procedure type for construction was not retrieved (the BMWE PDF could not be downloaded).
- All selection evidence is public-sector; no source on how SMEs select private tenders.

## 2. Time and cost per bid; split by activity; hit rate

### Takeaway
Measured cost per municipal bid is ca. EUR 900–1,600 in firms with 36–70 staff and ca. EUR 3,070 in an 8-person firm, and 49–89 % of it is the offer preparation itself (collecting material prices, comparing, calculating); formalities, pre-check and submission each cost only 1–7 % but cause most of the anger. Hit rates found: ca. 80 % (invited-only micro firm), ca. 30 % (mid-sized firm), "not recorded" (small firm); textbook figures of "up to 5 % of contract value" and "up to 20 bids per order" are old and unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **[STAT]** Cost per participation in one municipal tender: Company A (8 staff) ca. EUR 3,070; B (36 staff) ca. EUR 870–1,260; C (70 staff) ca. EUR 900–1,600. Larger firms are cheaper through scale, routine and because specialised clerks instead of the owner do parts of the work. — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 18–27](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf); summary in [handwerksblatt.de, 01.12.2024](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/baufirmen-sind-frustiert-wegen-vergabeverfahren)
- **[STAT]** Split of that cost by step (A / B / C): search – / 3–5 % / 12–22 %; pre-check 3 % / 1 % / 5–7 %; formalities (Nachweise, Erklärungen) 2 % / 2 % / 4–5 %; **offer preparation 89 % / 78–88 % / 49–63 %**; submission 2 % / 1–6 % / 3–6 %; Angebotsaufklärung – / 0–10 % / 0–22 %; overhead 4 % / 2–3 % / 1–2 %. — [IfM-Materialien 307, figs. 4–6](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** What "offer preparation" contains: "müssen zunächst verschiedene Angebote für das benötigte Material eingeholt, Preise verglichen und dann die eigenen Leistungen … kalkuliert werden"; errors "nachträglich nicht mehr korrigiert werden können"; pricing must be very tight because price has the highest weight. The study does **not** split this block further into reading / inquiring / calculating. — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 12](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** Older comparison cited by IfM: Fieseler et al. 2008 found on average EUR 731 per tender per firm across goods, construction and services. — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 27, fn. 17](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[PRACT]** Owner of Company A calculates at weekends because the needed quiet is not there during the week: "Das [Kalkulieren] ist eigentlich das aufreibendste. […] man darf sich nicht aus der Ruhe bringen lassen, weil wenn ich da 'nen Fehler mache [...], den krieg ich nicht mehr korrigiert, der Preis steht." — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 20–21](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[PRACT]** Company C on price breakdown forms (EFB-type Kalkulationsblätter): "Die Kalkulationsblätter sind einfach nervig, sind sehr zeitaufwendig. [...] Da musste jeder einzelne Einheitspreis noch aufgeschlüsselt und übermittelt werden und dann in deren Formular natürlich, nicht ein Ausdruck aus unserem Programm […] und da reden wir oftmals von 300 bis 800 Positionen." — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 26](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[PRACT]** Company C on product-neutral LVs: "Die Hölle sind produktneutrale Ausschreibungen. Man weiß selber nicht, welcher Hersteller dahintersteckt, versucht dann über Google viel zu lösen." and "Alte Produkte, die ausgeschrieben werden, da kann ich einige Produkte gar nicht mehr anbieten". — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 26](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT/PRACT]** Hit rates: Company A ca. 80 % (bids only when pointed to the tender); Company C "rund 30 %"; Company B: "Wie hoch die eigene Erfolgsquote … ist, wird von dem Unternehmen nicht erfasst." IfM's explanation: an invitation signals interest or few bidders. — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 18, 21, 24, 28](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[ACAD] (AT thesis 2009 citing Alznauer/Krafft 2004)** "Die Erstellung eines Angebots verursacht Kosten, die einen Anteil von bis zu 5% des erwarteten Auftragswertes ausmachen. Darüber hinaus müssen bis zu 20 Angebote abgegeben werden, um einen Auftrag zu erhalten." Upper bounds, 20+ years old, original not checked. — [Selberherr, TU Wien 2009, p. 24](https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/13599/2/Selberherr%20Julia%20-%202009%20-%20Angebotsstrategien%20unter%20Beruecksichtigung%20von...pdf)
- **[ACAD]** Bauprofessor defines two KPIs (Angebotserfolg I = orders won / bids submitted; II = order intake EUR / bid volume EUR) and gives no numbers: "Die absoluten Prozentzahlen für den Angebotserfolg schwanken jedoch zwischen einzelnen Bauunternehmen sehr stark" (by Hochbau/Tiefbau, trade, size, public/private). — [bauprofessor.de, Angebotserfolg](https://www.bauprofessor.de/angebotserfolg/)
- **[VENDOR, unsourced, self-labelled estimates]** "Angebotsbearbeitung kostet Bauunternehmen 2 bis 8 Arbeitstage pro Ausschreibung"; first analysis of the documents 3–6 hours; 3–5 bids per estimator per month; a 4-day bid ≈ EUR 1,200 at EUR 38/h. The same page states that the widely repeated 25–35 % hit rate has "Zitierketten ohne auffindbare Erhebung" and "eine belastbare Branchenzahl für die Trefferquote im deutschen Bauhauptgewerbe haben wir nicht gefunden." — [ki-syndikat.de](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/usecases/bauwesen/06-ausschreibung/)
- **[PRACT, vendor-adjacent editorial]** "Wer trotz der momentanen Nachfrage-Flut seine Angebote gewissenhaft kalkulieren will, kennt das: Ganze Abende gehen dafür drauf." — [bauprofessor.de news, 06.07.2019](https://www.bauprofessor.de/news/angebote-automatisch-kalkulieren/) (seen as a search snippet only)

### Inferences
- With EUR 900–1,600 per bid and a ca. 30 % hit rate (Company C), ca. EUR 3,000–5,300 of bid cost stands behind each won order; for 36–48 bids/year that is ca. EUR 32,000–77,000 of bidding cost per year (my arithmetic from the IfM figures; electrical trade, municipal lots — Tiefbau lots with site visits and subcontractor rounds will be higher).
- The IfM cost split says where hours go (calculation incl. price collection), the interviews say where the anger is (formalities, portals, submission). An assistant that only removes "bureaucracy" attacks 5–15 % of hours; one that shortens price collection and LV reading attacks the 50–90 % block.
- Company B not recording its hit rate at all, in a 36-person firm bidding 15–20 times a year, suggests that basic bid statistics are missing in the target segment.

### Gaps
- No German survey found (ZDB, HDB, BVMB, KfW, ifo, universities) with a representative **hit rate** or **bid cost as % of contract value / turnover** for Bauhauptgewerbe. The 25–35 % figure circulating on vendor sites has no traceable origin (stated by a vendor page itself).
- No measured split of the offer-preparation block into reading documents / price inquiries / calculating / filling forms. No hours-per-bid by tender size from a non-vendor source. No Tiefbau-specific measurement.
- BTU Cottbus thesis poster (Karas 2024) on Akquise appeared in search results but the file returned HTTP 404; not read.

## 3. Subcontractor and supplier inquiries during bidding

### Takeaway
Collecting material and subcontractor prices is part of every described workflow and every estimator job ad, and practitioners describe the last prices arriving under deadline pressure — but no source quantifies response rates or timing. "Waiting for prices" is described as a situation, not named as a stage.

### Cited Findings
- **[PRACT]** Company B, clerk who assembles the bid: "Wenn ich jetzt unter Zeitdruck bin und irgendwelche Sachen noch nicht da sind und der Meister jetzt z. B. die letzten Preise bekommt, die mit reingibt und ich dann in diesen komplizierten Programmen mit meinen Zetteln da rummache, dann ist das schon nervenaufreibend […]. Das setzt einen dann unter Druck." — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 22](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** Offer preparation starts with collecting "verschiedene Angebote für das benötigte Material" and comparing prices (the largest cost block, see section 2). — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 12](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[ACAD]** Listed preparatory work before the calculation: check of the tender documents; "Begehung der Baustelle bzw. Recherche der örtlichen Verhältnisse"; for work to be sublet "sind selbst Ausschreibungen vorzunehmen und Angebote einzuholen"; price information for materials and rentals; "Prüfung der im Leistungsverzeichnis angeführten Mengen, mindestens für die auftragsbestimmenden Leistungspositionen"; rough estimate of the bid total before calculating. — [bauprofessor.de, Vorarbeiten Angebotskalkulation](https://www.bauprofessor.de/vorarbeiten-angebotskalkulation/)
- **[JOBAD]** "Zusätzlich forderst Du Angebote von Nachunternehmern und Lieferanten an und prüfst diese sorgfältig" — [Hays, Kalkulator Tiefbau, Frankfurt](https://www.hays.de/jobsuche/stellenangebote-jobs-detail-kalkulator-tiefbau-frankfurt-am-main-879794/1)
- **[ACAD] (AT)** WKO guidance for subcontracting: a comparison of offers "ist … erst ab drei Bietern repräsentativ"; warns against awarding to long-standing partners without market check. — [WKO Know-how am Bau, Subvergabe](https://WKO.at/oe/gewerbe-handwerk/bau/knowhow-2--subvergabe-2.pdf) (search snippet, not opened)
- **[LAW/ACAD]** After submission, the client can demand the breakdown of subcontractor prices (Formblatt 223); risk that "die vorgenommene Aufgliederung nicht mit der Kalkulation des Nachunternehmers übereinstimmt". — [bauprofessor.de, Preisangaben zu Nachunternehmer](https://www.bauprofessor.de/preisangaben-zu-nachunternehmer/) (search snippet)
- **[ACAD] (AT)** Because of the low hit rate, the equipment actually used is not fixed at bid time, "Daher muss mit Durchschnittswerten kalkuliert werden, wodurch die Gefahr unzureichender Kostensätze erhöht wird." — [Selberherr, TU Wien 2009](https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/13599/2/Selberherr%20Julia%20-%202009%20-%20Angebotsstrategien%20unter%20Beruecksichtigung%20von...pdf)

### Inferences
- The one direct practitioner quote on this point describes exactly the state "bid is assembled, last prices still missing, deadline close" and a second person (the Meister) feeding prices in at the end. This is a parallel sub-state of "Pricing", not a stage after it: calculation of own work and waiting for third-party prices run at the same time.
- If three comparable offers are the stated minimum and suppliers also see a low chance that the inquiring bidder wins, low and late response is plausible — but this is inference, not found evidence.

### Gaps
- **No figures found** on: how inquiries are sent (e-mail with GAEB/PDF extract vs. portal vs. phone), number of inquiries per bid, share answered before the deadline, how many days before the deadline prices arrive. Searches for surveys (incl. vendor surveys such as Cosuno) returned nothing.
- No practitioner source found that uses a term such as "Preise ausstehend" as a named status. No forum thread (Reddit, bauexpertenforum, tiefbauforum) on this could be retrieved; searches for Reddit threads returned none.

## 4. Bidder questions: asked or not, and speculation instead

### Takeaway
Lawyers say: ask — answers go to all bidders, there is no "too late" question, and a bidder who sees a gap and prices the favourable reading loses the later Nachtrag. Practitioner and academic sources show the opposite practice exists: bid exactly what is written even when known to be wrong, and weight items with change-order potential; a TU Dresden thesis with a contractor concludes that the price-only award "forces" bidders into these strategies.

### Cited Findings
- **[PRACT]** Owner, Company A, on a wrong LV: "Ja, was sie ausgeschrieben haben, passt gar nicht. Dann rege ich mich dann wieder auf und sage: Wozu haben die Fachplaner dran? Bist du jetzt ehrlich und rechnest es rein? Kriegst du es nicht. Dann bietet es nur das an, was ausgeschrieben ist. Du weißt von vornherein, es ist falsch, aber ich biete es an, weil ich mich strikt nach der Ausschreibung halte." — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 20](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[PRACT]** Same owner on clauses shifting planning responsibility to the bidder: "Ich biete das an, was in der Ausschreibung steht, und wenn die mir nachher einen Strick draus drehen wollen, weil ich mich an die Ausschreibung gehalten habe, nur weil dieser Satz drinsteht: 'Ich muss selber fachlich prüfen, ob alles so richtig ist'. … Weil die kriegen richtig Geld dafür, die Fachplaner, und dann sollen die auch dafür gerade stehen." — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 20](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[STAT]** In Company B, "Rückfragen bei der Vergabestelle" are among the steps that are **not** gone through in every tender (they appear only in the upper cost bound). — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 21](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[ACAD, with contractor Hentschke Bau as co-supervisor]** "Es konnte gezeigt werden, dass die strategischen Ansätze der Bieter in der Angebotslegung für ihn notwendig sind, um das Ziel des Zuschlags und der Kostendeckung zu erreichen. Es wurde entsprechend Kritik am Vergabeverfahren geäußert, welches den Bieter zum Einsatz dieser Strategien zwingt." (strategies = "die im Allgemeinen als Spekulation betitelten strategischen Ansätze") — [Hesse, Diplomarbeit TU Dresden 2022, poster](https://tu-dresden.de/bu/bauingenieurwesen/ibb/ressourcen/dateien/publikationen/diplomarbeiten/poster_da_2022/Hesse_Kurt-Georg_Poster_DA_2022.pdf)
- **[PRACT/trade press] (CH, 2013)** Describes the tactic openly: defects and contradictions with "Nachtragspotenzial" are recorded during the offer check; "Positionen, die eine Grundlage für mögliche Nachtragsabrechnungen bilden, werden von vielen Unternehmen kostenmässig als hohe Budgetposten gewichtet. Positionen ohne Potenzial für Nachträge hingegen müssten kostenmässig geringer bewertet werden. Dadurch bleibt sich der Angebotspreis gleich." … "Diese Spekulation wird zu einem Verlust, sobald …" (expected quantities do not occur). — [Baublatt Nr. 13, 28.03.2013](https://www.baublatt.ch/file/1041/2bb1313nachhaltigkeitsmanagement.pdf)
- **[LAW]** Dr. Reinhard Voppel (Osenbrück Bubert Kirsten Voppel, Köln), on a roofer's rejected Nachtrag for fall protection: the bidder "müsse die Zweifelsfragen vor Angebotsabgabe durch Rückfrage beim Auftraggeber klären." "Insbesondere dürfe der Bieter nicht den für ihn günstigeren Fall der Kalkulation zugrunde legen." Court and file number not given in the article. — [ingenieur.de / HLH, 18.09.2026](https://www.ingenieur.de/fachmedien/hlh/baurecht-kalkulationsrisiko-bei-unklarer-leistungsbeschreibung/)
- **[LAW] conflicting line (search summary only, page returned 403)** A Wolters Kluwer expert article reports, citing BGH 12.09.2013 – VII ZR 227/11, that a bidder is not obliged to point out unclear points or gaps before bidding and that a bidder question is solely in the bidder's own interest. — [Wolters Kluwer, 14.09.2021](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/de-de/expert-insights/oeffentliche-auftragsvergabe-1); contrasts with [ingenieur.de, 18.09.2026](https://www.ingenieur.de/fachmedien/hlh/baurecht-kalkulationsrisiko-bei-unklarer-leistungsbeschreibung/)
- **[LAW]** BGH 13.09.2022 – XIII ZR 9/20: a bidder who misreads the LV and prices something not asked for does not offer the required price; exclusion under §§ 13, 16 VOB/A. — [BGH via Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/fileadmin/Aktuelles/BGH_13.09.2022_XIII_ZR_9-20_1328.pdf) (search snippet)
- **[LAW/ACAD]** Questions may be asked "bis kurz vor Ablauf der Angebotsfrist"; VK Bund 27.01.2017 (VK 2-131/16): no "zu späten Bieterfragen" — the authority can extend the deadline; answers must be made available to all bidders. — [bauprofessor.de, Bieterfragen vor Angebot](https://www.bauprofessor.de/bieterfragen-vor-angebot/)
- **[LAW]** Know-how leak: "kann auf konkrete Angebotsinhalte der Mitbewerber geschlossen werden, wenn diese zum Inhalt von Bieterfragen werden"; answers go to all bidders under transparency and equal treatment. — [Vergabeblog, 07.12.2017](https://vergabeblog.de/2017-12-07/die-zulaessigkeit-der-abaenderung-von-bieterfragen-durch-die-vergabestelle/) (search snippet)
- **[LAW]** The line between a Bieterfrage and a Rüge is "nicht einfach und außerordentlich wichtig"; a bidder who accepts a visible ambiguity may later be unable to rely on it; defects raised only after submission can forfeit rights. — [Vergabeblog, 13.01.2025](https://vergabeblog.de/2025-01-13/zum-richtigen-umgang-mit-fragen-und-ruegen-im-vergabeverfahren/) (snippet); [Vergabeblog, 22.06.2026](https://vergabeblog.de/2026-06-22/vergabeverfahren-typische-fehlerquellen-in-der-angebotsphase-und-wie-unternehmen-sie-vermeiden/)
- **[LAW]** Do not "clarify" in the cover letter instead: "Schon eine vermeintlich harmlose Klarstellung kann als unzulässige Änderung der Vergabeunterlagen gewertet werden" — ask a question instead. — [Vergabeblog, 22.06.2026](https://vergabeblog.de/2026-06-22/vergabeverfahren-typische-fehlerquellen-in-der-angebotsphase-und-wie-unternehmen-sie-vermeiden/)
- **[VENDOR]** ibau lists "Clarification questions asked only after the bid deadline" among formal defects and warns "Wer nicht frühzeitig fragt, läuft Gefahr, dass sein Angebot ausgeschlossen wird." — [ibau glossary](https://www.ibau.de/akademie/glossar/formelle-maengel-von-angeboten/); [ibau, Häufige Fehler](https://www.ibau.de/akademie/wissenswertes/fehler-bei-der-teilnahme-an-oeffentlichen-ausschreibungen/)

### Inferences
- "Questions" is not a stage every bid passes through. In the measured SME (Company B) it is occasional; in Company A the stated behaviour is to bid the LV as written and keep quiet. Practitioners would more likely recognise it as a **decision per finding** (ask / price in / keep as change-order potential / leave) inside the check-and-pricing phase than as a column a tender sits in.
- The practitioner's own reasoning for not asking, in the one verbatim case, is price competition ("Bist du jetzt ehrlich und rechnest es rein? Kriegst du es nicht") — pricing the correct scope loses the job; asking would hand the same correction to all bidders. Fear of annoying the client and lack of time were **not** found as stated reasons in any source.
- The legal risk of the silent route is concrete and current (Voppel 2026): a recognised gap that was not asked about can kill the Nachtrag. So each LV finding has two money values: the change-order potential if silent and it holds, and the loss if a court says "you had to ask". A tool that records, per finding, that the ambiguity was seen and what was decided creates evidence — useful for the Bauleiter later, and legally double-edged (it documents that the gap was recognised).

### Gaps
- No survey or statistic found on **how often SMEs ask bidder questions** or their reasons for not asking. No German practitioner quote found that names "the answer goes to all competitors" as the reason (only lawyers describe the mechanism).
- The Wolters Kluwer article and BGH VII ZR 227/11 were not read in full; the apparent conflict with the 2026 decision reported by Voppel is unresolved here (likely: no general duty to warn, but the risk of the favourable reading stays with the bidder).
- Görres' dissertation (UniBw 2015, 496 pp., large projects) on disruption factors in the bid phase was downloaded but keyword search gave no usable passage on bidder questions.

## 5. Procedural reasons for exclusion and lost bids

### Takeaway
The failure modes are well documented by lawyers and procurement chambers (late upload by minutes, wrong upload address, missing e-signature, missing forms/Nachweise, own terms or "clarifications" that count as changing the documents, unfilled or contradictory price items), and practitioners name the submission moment as their peak stress. ### Inferences
- A distinct step between "Pricing" and "Submitted" is visible in every source: **completing and checking the bid package** (forms, Nachweise, signatures, price breakdown sheets, format per portal, upload with buffer). ## 6. After submission

### Takeaway
Practitioners name two emotional peaks: submitting on time and the Submissionsergebnis. After that the public process can add Nachforderung of documents, an Angebotsaufklärung (rare, but 10–22 % of bid cost when it happens, plus travel), months of delay with requests to extend the Bindefrist, and no explanation of why the bid lost. Evidence that SMEs analyse lost bids systematically was not found; one firm does not even record its hit rate.

- **[PRACT]** Company C: "Wenn es um Emotionen geht, gibt es für mich nur 2,3 Momente. Das ist der eine Moment, pünktlich abzugeben [..]. Da muss dann schon mal gefragt werden … 'Wie kann das sein, dass du 20 % teurer bist wie der andere?'" — [IfM-Materialien 307, p. 20](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- Between "Submitted" and "Award" there are at least three deadline-bearing events the board does not show: **Submissionsergebnis known (rank and gap to first)**, **Nachforderung / Aufklärung requested (answer due)**, **Bindefrist ends / extension requested (decide: hold the price or drop out)**. Each needs an action and a date; "Submitted" as one silent column hides them.
- Rank after opening is the practitioner's real status ("we are second, 4 % behind"), known weeks or months before "Award". The existence of a paid market for submission results one hour after opening shows how much contractors and their suppliers want this number.
- For private clients and GUs the stage after submission is negotiation (Vergabegespräch, discount rounds); job ads confirm estimators attend these, but nothing found describes the rounds.
- A lost-bid record (our price, winning price, rank, number of bidders, client) is the cheapest missing data set: it would give Company B its hit rate and feed the next bid/no-bid decision.

### Gaps
- No source found on whether SMEs **systematically analyse lost bids**; no survey on attendance at / reading of opening results since e-Vergabe; no data on typical time from submission to award or on frequency of Bindefrist extensions.
- No practitioner account found of negotiation rounds and discounts with private clients / GUs in Germany.

## 7. Handover after winning: Kalkulation → Bauleitung

### Takeaway
Textbook and trade-press sources say a handover talk between estimator and site management should pass on risks and change-order chances found during bidding, and list "non-binding handover talks" and "no Arbeitskalkulation" as known weak points; job ads show the estimator stays involved (Arbeitskalkulation, Nachträge with the Bauleitung, Nachkalkulation). No German study quantifying what is lost in the handover was found.

### Cited Findings
- **[PRACT/trade press] (CH, 2013)** "Zur Früherkennung des Nachtragspotenzials und etwaiger Risiken in der Ausführungsphase sollte der Kalkulator und Angebotsprojektleiter mit dem Baustellenchef und der Arbeitsvorbereitung (AVOR) ein Übergabegespräch führen. Dies, um das Baustellenteam auf Gefahrenherde sowie auf Chancen möglicher Nachträge in der Ausführungsphase hinzuweisen. Dabei geht es darum, dem Team gewichtete Abrechnungspositionen vor Augen zu führen, damit diese bei Abweichungen die richtigen Massnahmen treffen können." What should be recognised already in the bid phase: which items will have more or less quantity, where obstruction from planning or site release will arise. Named internal loss-makers: "unzureichende oder keine Arbeitskalkulation", missing AVOR, missing target hours. — [Baublatt Nr. 13, 28.03.2013](https://www.baublatt.ch/file/1041/2bb1313nachhaltigkeitsmanagement.pdf)
- **[ACAD]** A student summary of site-management weak points lists "Unverbindliche Übergabegespräche"; also: IT often not integrated, technical and commercial data cannot be matched. — [buffl.co study notes](https://buffl.co/community/c/66af7192a14654b156ad4dd8) (search snippet; low-grade source)

- **[JOBAD]** "Unterstützung bei der Erstellung von Arbeitskalkulationen"; "Erstellung von Nachträgen in Zusammenarbeit mit der Bauleitung"; "Ausarbeitung von Sondervorschlägen und Nebenangeboten"; "Pflege der Grunddaten der Kalkulation"; "Mengenermittlung im Rahmen der Ausschreibung". — [Hays, Kalkulator Tiefbau, Dortmund](https://www.hays.de/jobsuche/stellenangebote-jobs-detail-kalkulator-tiefbau-dortmund-858140/1)

### Inferences
- The findings a "Check" stage produces (risks, quantity doubts, change-order potential, open questions and their answers) are exactly what the handover talk is supposed to carry. If the tool keeps them per item, the award can generate the handover sheet automatically — a stage **after "Award"** ("Handover") that practitioners already know by name (Übergabegespräch, Arbeitskalkulation).
- Job ads consistently describe the estimator's work as not ending at submission: award talks, Arbeitskalkulation, Nachträge, Nachkalkulation. A board ending at "Award" cuts off the part where bid-phase knowledge earns money.

### Gaps
- No German empirical source (survey, interviews) on how often handover talks happen, how they are documented, or what information is lost. The "knowledge is lost" claim rests on prescriptive literature plus one low-grade weak-point list.
- Recurring task bullets from job ads: only three Hays ads could be read in full (stellenmarkt.de returned 429, join.com 410, Trier PDF 404). Recurring across those read/snippeted: evaluate tender documents; quantity take-off / check; calculate and prepare the offer; request and check subcontractor and supplier offers; take part in award/contract talks; Arbeitskalkulation; Nachträge with the Bauleitung; Nachkalkulation; maintain master data; Sondervorschläge/Nebenangebote. "Preisspiegel" and "Übergabe an die Bauleitung" as literal bullets were not confirmed.

- **[STAT]** General shortage: in the DIHK survey 60 % of construction firms still named Fachkräftemangel as a business risk (HDB Brancheninfo, Feb 2026, capacity situation slightly eased). — [Hauptverband der Deutschen Bauindustrie, Fachkräftesituation im Bauhauptgewerbe](https://www.bauindustrie.de/fileadmin/bauindustrie.de/Zahlen_Fakten/BrancheninfoBau/BiB_-_Fachkraeftesituation_im_Bauhauptgewerbe.pdf) (search snippet)
- **[STAT]** PwC 2025: 82 % of firms lack the knowledge to use the potential of digitalisation. — [PwC press release 2025](https://www.pwc.de/de/pressemitteilungen/2025/bauindustrie-im-digital-und-esg-dilemma-buerokratie-und-fachkraeftemangel-als-wachstumsbremsen.html) (search snippet)
- **[JOBAD]** Demand signal: recruiters run many parallel "Kalkulator Tiefbau (m/w/d)" searches (Frankfurt, Dortmund, Ludwigsfelde, Kelsterbach); requirements: engineer/Techniker, RIB iTWO or ARRIBA, VOB basics; one ad quotes EUR 70,000–80,000 p.a. for a Kalkulator/Abrechner Tiefbau. — [Hays, Frankfurt](https://www.hays.de/jobsuche/stellenangebote-jobs-detail-kalkulator-tiefbau-frankfurt-am-main-879794/1); [remotely.de listing](https://www.remotely.de/job/riverstate-premium-recruiting-kalkulator-abrechner-tiefbau-mwd-70000-80000-pa) (search snippet)
- **[VENDOR, estimate]** 3–5 bids per estimator per month. — [ki-syndikat.de](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/usecases/bauwesen/06-ausschreibung/)
- **[LAW]** Recommended against missed deadlines: an internal deadline well before the official one, a timeline, one owner per requirement, central document storage. — [Vergabeblog, 22.06.2026](https://vergabeblog.de/2026-06-22/vergabeverfahren-typische-fehlerquellen-in-der-angebotsphase-und-wie-unternehmen-sie-vermeiden/)

### Inferences
- In SMEs below ca. 50 staff the "Kalkulator" is often not a full-time role: it is the owner or Meister, with office staff doing forms and upload. The board's user is therefore two roles — the person who prices and the person who assembles and submits — and the hand-off between them ("last prices from the Meister") is where the deadline stress sits.
- The dates a bid carries (question deadline, site visit, internal deadline, submission, opening, Nachforderung answer, Bindefrist end, planned start) are more than one per tender; a board that shows only the stage loses them.

### Gaps
- No source found on **how deadlines are tracked today** (Excel, Outlook, whiteboard) — the paper-notes quotes are about portal handling, not deadline lists. No statistic on a shortage of estimators as a profession, vacancy durations, or bids handled in parallel per person.
- Practitioner forums and Reddit: targeted searches returned no usable threads; LinkedIn posts by estimators were not searched.

## 9. Surveys on digitalisation / AI in bid preparation and calculation (2022–2026)

### Takeaway
No survey was found that asks German construction firms which bid-preparation steps they want automated. What exists: general expectation figures (PwC: 80 % see high AI potential in 2026; 82 % lacked digital know-how in 2025), vendor surveys of trades, a thesis claim of 20–40 % less routine work in first-pass calculation, and practitioners' own wish list in the IfM interviews (once-only Nachweise, one portal, one format, human support at submission).

- **[STAT]** PwC 2026 study (150 construction firms, planners and suppliers): "80 Prozent der Befragten schreiben KI hohes Potenzial zu"; PwC 2025: 66 % say the future belongs to AI-based technologies; 82 % lack the knowledge to exploit digitalisation. Expectations, not use; no breakdown by bid step in what was accessible. — [PwC study 2026 (page returned 403; via search summary)](https://www.pwc.de/de/risk-regulatory/risk/capital-projects-and-infrastructure/pwc-studie-2026-zur-deutschen-bauindustrie.html); [PwC press release 2025](https://www.pwc.de/de/pressemitteilungen/2025/bauindustrie-im-digital-und-esg-dilemma-buerokratie-und-fachkraeftemangel-als-wachstumsbremsen.html)

- **[VENDOR survey, trades DE/AT]** plancraft (1,026 craft firms): every third firm uses AI daily; 71 % say AI helps them take on more orders or process them faster. Company C: in-house digitalisation ("dass wir die Sachen schon viele Jahre einlesen können, mit Datenträgern arbeiten können") already made submission easier. — [IfM-Materialien 307, pp. 23–24, 26–27, Kurzfassung](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[VENDOR, self-critical]** A vendor page on AI tender analysis states it found "keine unabhängige Untersuchung" comparing tender analysis with and without AI, and that the "bis zu 80 Prozent" time saving comes from vendor material. — [ki-syndikat.de](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/usecases/bauwesen/06-ausschreibung/)

### Inferences
- The practitioners' own wish list is about the low-hour, high-anger steps (Nachweise, portals, formats, upload); the academic and vendor pitch is about the high-hour step (first-pass calculation, LV analysis). Both are open; nobody in the sources found asks for automated bidder questions, supplier chasing or lost-bid analysis — these are unoccupied in the evidence, which is either an opening or a sign of low perceived need.
- Any time-saving number for AI in bid preparation currently available in German is vendor-originated; a measured before/after on real LVs would be new evidence.

### Gaps
- No survey by ZDB, HDB, BVMB, Bitkom, BRZ, KfW, ifo or BBSR found on AI/digitalisation **by step** of Angebotsbearbeitung. PwC full reports (2025, 2026 PDFs) were not readable in this pass and may contain use-case breakdowns.
- Fraunhofer IRB's AI survey found is about Bausachverständige, not contractors' bidding.

## Cross-cutting: would practitioners recognise the five board stages?

(Placed under the last key question as synthesis; all points are inferences from the cited findings above.)

### Takeaway
"Pricing/Kalkulation", "Submitted/Abgabe" and "Award/Zuschlag" match the practitioners' and IfM's vocabulary; "Check" exists as work but is done inside calculation, and "Questions" is an occasional action, not a stage. Missing against the evidence: a stage before Check (found / invited → go/no-go), a completeness-and-upload gate before Submitted, the opening result and clarification events between Submitted and Award, and a handover after Award.

### Cited Findings
- **[STAT]** The process as IfM modelled it with the firms: (1) Suche nach Ausschreibungen, (2) Vorabprüfung / erste Prüfung der Anforderungen, (3) Zusammenstellung der Formalien, (4) Angebotserstellung, (5) Angebotsabgabe, (6) Angebotsaufklärung, then Entscheidung der Vergabestelle. — [IfM-Materialien 307, section 3.2 and figs. 4–6](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/fileadmin/data/redaktion/publikationen/ifm_materialien/dokumente/IfM-Materialien-307_2024.pdf)
- **[ACAD]** Textbook order of the estimator's preparatory work: check documents → site visit → own inquiries to subcontractors/suppliers → quantity check of the order-defining items → rough estimate → calculation. — [bauprofessor.de](https://www.bauprofessor.de/vorarbeiten-angebotskalkulation/)

### Inferences
- Mapping, with the evidence for each:
  - *(missing)* **Found / invited → go/no-go** — IfM steps 1–2; 12–22 % of cost at Company C; capacity- and region-driven.
  - **Check** — recognised as "Prüfung der Ausschreibungsunterlagen … Chancen und Risiken" (job ads); includes site visit and quantity check, which the board does not show.
  - **Questions** — occasional; better a per-finding decision (ask / price in / hold as Nachtragspotenzial) with the answer deadline as a date, than a column.
  - **Pricing** — the 49–89 % block; contains the unnamed sub-state "waiting for supplier/subcontractor prices".
  - *(missing)* **Package complete / ready to submit** — IfM steps 3 and 5; forms, Nachweise, price breakdown sheets (300–800 items), signature, format, upload with buffer.
  - **Submitted** — then: opening result (rank, gap), Nachforderung, Aufklärung, Bindefrist extension; private clients: negotiation rounds.
  - **Award** — or lost (with reason and winning price recorded).
  - *(missing)* **Handover** — Übergabegespräch, Arbeitskalkulation, findings passed to the Bauleitung.
- Most painful by hours: pricing including price collection. Most painful by emotion: repeated Nachweise/forms, portal and format chaos, the last 15 minutes before upload, and losing on price without explanation. Most dangerous by money: a recognised LV gap handled silently (lost Nachtrag) and a calculation error that cannot be corrected after submission.

### Gaps
- No practitioner source was found that describes its own pipeline stages or a bid board (Kanban, Excel list, whiteboard); stage vocabulary here comes from a research institute's model, textbooks and job ads. Validation with two or three estimators (incl. Tiefbau) remains necessary — the project's civil-engineer contact is the obvious first check.
