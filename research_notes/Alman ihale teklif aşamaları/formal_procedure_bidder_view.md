# Formal procedure of a German construction tender from the bidder's point of view (VOB/A below and above the EU threshold; private clients)

Research date: 2026-11-21. Reading notes on source quality:

- Law text was read on dejure.org (VOB/A section 1 = "Fassung 2019"; EU paragraphs shown by dejure as "Fassung 2026"; GWB as in force since 01.07.2026). Paragraph contents below are paraphrases of the fetched pages with short literal quotes; re-check the wording before quoting it in a product.
- VHB Bund was read directly from the official "Lesefassung" PDF on fib-bund.de (footers say "Ausgabe 2017 – Stand 2019", some forms "Stand 2023"). I could not confirm whether the VHB has been updated to VOB/A 2026.
- Items marked "(search snippet only)" come from a search-result extract, not from a page I read in full.
- Abbreviations: AG = Auftraggeber (client), VK = Vergabekammer, KT = Kalendertage.

## 1. Before bidding: notice, procedure types, which of them add a stage before the bid, Präqualifikation

### Takeaway
Only three of the procedure types put a formal stage in front of the bid: Beschränkte Ausschreibung mit Teilnahmewettbewerb (national) and nicht offenes Verfahren / Verhandlungsverfahren mit Teilnahmewettbewerb (EU) — there the bidder first files a Teilnahmeantrag, is checked for Eignung, and only then receives the Aufforderung zur Angebotsabgabe. In Beschränkte Ausschreibung ohne Teilnahmewettbewerb and Freihändige Vergabe the bidder does not apply at all: the client picks firms and invites them, so the first event is an unsolicited invitation.

### Cited Findings
- National procedure types (§ 3 VOB/A): Öffentliche Ausschreibung = public call to an unlimited number of firms ("nach öffentlicher Aufforderung einer unbeschränkten Zahl von Unternehmen zur Einreichung von Angeboten"); Beschränkte Ausschreibung (with or without Teilnahmewettbewerb) = only a limited number of firms is invited to bid; Freihändige Vergabe = "in einem vereinfachten Verfahren". — [dejure § 3 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/3.html)
- Free choice between Öffentliche Ausschreibung and Beschränkte Ausschreibung mit Teilnahmewettbewerb; the other types only under conditions. Value limits shown by dejure with "Stand 01.01.2026": Beschränkte Ausschreibung ohne Teilnahmewettbewerb up to 150,000 EUR net; Freihändige Vergabe up to 100,000 EUR net; Direktauftrag (no award procedure at all) up to 50,000 EUR net. — [dejure § 3a VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/3a.html)
- These limits are new since 1 January 2026 (amendment of § 3a of section 1, published in the Bundesanzeiger 16.12.2025, BAnz AT 16.12.2025 B7; unlimited in time; the earlier raised limits had been temporary until 31.12.2025). The Länder may set other values (Rheinland-Pfalz explicitly does not apply them). (search snippets only) — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/vergabe-am-bau-2026-gelten-deutlich-hoehere-wertgrenzen); [cosinex](https://blog.cosinex.de/2025/12/19/erhoehte-wertgrenzen-fuer-bauleistungen-ab-1-januar/); [Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/de/news-events/newsletter-fachbeitraege/artikel/neue-wertgrenzen-fuer-bauauftraege.html)
- Timing of the Eignungsprüfung (§ 6b VOB/A): in Beschränkte Ausschreibung and Freihändige Vergabe the suitability is checked "vor der Aufforderung zur Angebotsabgabe"; in Beschränkte Ausschreibung mit Teilnahmewettbewerb the Eigenerklärungen/Nachweise are due "bereits mit dem Teilnahmeantrag"; in Öffentliche Ausschreibung the invitation states which proofs come with the bid and which are requested later. — [dejure § 6b VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/6b.html)
- Bewerbungsfrist (deadline for the Teilnahmeantrag) below the threshold: "eine ausreichende Bewerbungsfrist", no number of days. — [dejure § 10 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10.html)
- EU procedure flow (§ 3b EU VOB/A): offenes Verfahren — any firm may bid; nicht offenes Verfahren — public Teilnahmewettbewerb, only selected applicants may bid, the minimum number of invited firms is five; Verhandlungsverfahren mit Teilnahmewettbewerb — minimum three, selected firms submit an Erstangebot, negotiations on Erst- and Folgeangebote (minimum requirements and award criteria are not negotiable), possibly in successive phases with a shrinking field, at the end one common deadline for endgültige Angebote; the client may award on the Erstangebote without negotiating if it reserved this; Verhandlungsverfahren ohne Teilnahmewettbewerb — the client approaches firms directly. Wettbewerblicher Dialog and Innovationspartnerschaft also start with a Teilnahmewettbewerb (min. three). — [dejure § 3b EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/3b_EU.html)
- EU deadlines for the application stage (§ 10b EU): Teilnahmefrist at least 30 KT from the day after sending the notice; then Angebotsfrist at least 30 KT from the day after sending the Aufforderung zur Angebotsabgabe; urgency: 15 KT / 10 KT. — [dejure § 10b EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10b_EU.html)
- EU threshold for works in 2026 and 2027: 5,404,000 EUR (previously 5,538,000 EUR for 2024/2025), from Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2152, valid 01.01.2026–31.12.2027. (search snippet only) — [IHK Hannover](https://www.ihk.de/hannover/hauptnavigation/unternehmensfoerderung/oeffentliches-auftragswesen/aktuell-auftragswesen/neue-schwellenwerte-fuer-eu-ausschreibungen-fuer-2026-und-2027-6927176)
- Präqualifikation: proof of suitability can be given by entry in the "allgemein zugängliche Liste des Vereins für die Präqualifikation von Bauunternehmen e. V." which the client retrieves directly; alternatively single proofs, or Eigenerklärungen as "vorläufiger Nachweis" which bidders in the engere Wahl must later confirm with certificates. — [dejure § 6b VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/6b.html)
- PQ-VOB in practice: the entry stays valid as long as the underlying proofs are current; clients applying VOB/A must accept it; the bidder compiles the documents once for any number of tenders. (search snippets only; sources partly run by PQ bodies, not neutral) — [cosinex](https://blog.cosinex.de/2025/02/04/praequalifikation-alles-was-vergabestellen-und-bieter-wissen-muessen/); [bauprofessor](https://www.bauprofessor.de/praequalifikation-vob/)
- VHB Formblatt 216 "Verzeichnis der im Vergabeverfahren vorzulegenden Unterlagen" splits the documents into those due with the bid and those "die auf Verlangen der Vergabestelle vorzulegen sind": Formblatt 223, references, Gewerbeanmeldung/Handelsregister/Handwerksrolle, Unbedenklichkeitsbescheinigungen (Sozialkasse, Finanzamt, Berufsgenossenschaft), Freistellungsbescheinigung § 48b EStG, product data sheets, Urkalkulation. — [VHB Bund Lesefassung, Formblatt 216](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- States before "Check" that the board lacks: "Bekanntmachung seen / interested", "Teilnahmeantrag in preparation", "Teilnahmeantrag submitted", "not selected (abgelehnter Bewerber)", "invited (Aufforderung zur Angebotsabgabe received)". For invited procedures the entry point is the invitation itself.
- A PQ entry removes most of the "documents on request" work after submission; a firm without PQ will see more § 16a-type requests.

### Gaps
- Exact content duties of the Bekanntmachung (§ 12 VOB/A, § 12 EU) were not read.
- Länder value limits differ; no state-by-state table collected here.
- Whether the 50,000 EUR Direktauftrag limit quoted by GvW for the federal level (via BHO) and the § 3a Abs. 4 VOB/A value are the same rule or two parallel rules was not clarified.

## 2. During the bid period: Angebotsfrist, bidder questions, addenda, Hinweispflicht, Rüge, withdrawal

### Takeaway
Below the threshold the bid period only has a floor of 10 calendar days; above it the standard is 35 days (open) or 30 + 30 days (restricted). Three duties run in parallel with pricing: ask questions early (answers go to all bidders; above the threshold they must be out 6 days before the deadline), tell the client in text form about unclear or wrong tender documents before bidding (VHB Teilnahmebedingungen), and — above the threshold — file a Rüge for visible defects before the bid deadline, otherwise the right to a review is lost.

### Cited Findings
- § 10 Abs. 1 VOB/A: "eine ausreichende Angebotsfrist ... auch bei Dringlichkeit nicht unter zehn Kalendertagen"; site visits and procurement of documents must be allowed for. — [dejure § 10 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10.html)
- § 10a EU (open procedure): Angebotsfrist at least 35 KT from the day after sending the notice; 15 KT with Vorinformation or urgency; minus 5 KT if electronic bids are accepted (i.e. 30 KT in practice); plus 5 KT if documents are not freely accessible electronically. — [dejure § 10a EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10a_EU.html)
- § 10b EU (restricted procedure): Teilnahmefrist ≥ 30 KT; Angebotsfrist ≥ 30 KT (25 with electronic submission; 10 with Vorinformation; urgency 15/10). — [dejure § 10b EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10b_EU.html)
- Mandatory extension above the threshold: additional information must be available to all firms at the latest 6 KT before the deadline (4 KT in urgency); if not, or if the tender documents are changed substantially, the Angebotsfrist must be extended in proportion. — [dejure § 10a EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10a_EU.html)
- Bidder questions below the threshold (§ 12a Abs. 4 VOB/A): "Erbitten Unternehmen zusätzliche sachdienliche Auskünfte über die Vergabeunterlagen, so sind diese Auskünfte allen Unternehmen unverzüglich in gleicher Weise zu erteilen." No deadline in days. The names of firms that received documents are secret (Abs. 3). — [dejure § 12a VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/12a.html)
- Hinweispflicht in the VHB Teilnahmebedingungen (Formblatt 212, No. 1 "Mitteilung von Unklarheiten in den Vergabeunterlagen"): "Enthalten die Vergabeunterlagen nach Auffassung des Unternehmens Unklarheiten, Unvollständigkeiten oder Fehler, so hat es unverzüglich die Vergabestelle vor Angebotsabgabe in Textform darauf hinzuweisen." — [VHB Bund Lesefassung, Formblatt 212](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Opposing view on the reach of that duty: a Wolters Kluwer article argues the bidder is not obliged to point out gaps in the Leistungsbeschreibung before bidding; clients nevertheless raise this objection in later disputes. A CMS article shows exclusion risk when contradictions in the bid cannot be cleared up. (search snippets only) — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/de-de/expert-insights/oeffentliche-auftragsvergabe-1); [CMS](https://cms.law/de/deu/publication/keine-zweite-chance-kann-ein-bieter-widerspruechliche-nicht-aufklaeren-ist-eine-weitere-aufklaerung-unzulaessig)
- Rüge above the threshold (§ 160 Abs. 3 GWB, version in force since 01.07.2026): the review application is inadmissible if (Nr. 1) a recognised violation was not objected to "innerhalb einer Frist von zehn Kalendertagen"; (Nr. 2) violations visible in the Bekanntmachung were not objected to by the end of the Bewerbungs-/Angebotsfrist; (Nr. 3) violations visible in the Vergabeunterlagen were not objected to by the end of the Bewerbungs-/Angebotsfrist; (Nr. 4) "mehr als 15 Kalendertage nach Eingang der Mitteilung des Auftraggebers, einer Rüge nicht abhelfen zu wollen" have passed. — [dejure § 160 GWB](https://dejure.org/gesetze/GWB/160.html)
- Below the threshold a Rüge is not written in statute, but courts expect it: OLG Zweibrücken (13.09.2021, 1 U 93/20) treats a prior Rüge as required; the author advises prompt written objections. — [Vergabeblog on LG Aurich 2 O 98/26](https://vergabeblog.de/2026-07-27/effektiver-eilrechtsschutz-bei-bauvergaben-im-unterschwellenbereich/)
- Withdrawal: "Bis zum Ablauf der Angebotsfrist können Angebote in Textform zurückgezogen werden" (§ 10 Abs. 2 VOB/A; identical § 10a EU Abs. 7, § 10b EU Abs. 7). After that the Bindefrist runs ("Die Bindefrist beginnt mit dem Ablauf der Angebotsfrist"). — [dejure § 10 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10.html); [dejure § 10a EU](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10a_EU.html)
- Error after the deadline: if the bidder invokes an Irrtum and the supervising level accepts the Anfechtung, "ist das Angebot hinfällig ... Eine Änderung des angeblich irrig angegebenen Preises ist nicht zulässig." — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321 Nr. 7](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- Missing events between "Check"/"Questions" and "Pricing": "answers to bidder questions received (also other bidders' questions)", "addendum / changed tender documents received (new LV version)", "Angebotsfrist extended", "Rüge sent / client's answer (Abhilfe or Nichtabhilfe → 15-day clock to the VK)", "bid withdrawn / replaced before deadline".
- For an LV-checking tool the Hinweispflicht and the Rüge deadline (end of the bid period for anything visible in the documents) turn findings into dated obligations: a defect found in "Check" must be reported before submission to keep rights.

### Gaps
- No explicit below-threshold rule found on the latest date for asking questions; clients set a cut-off in the tender documents (not sourced here).
- The rule on changing (not withdrawing) a submitted bid before the deadline was not read in the law text (§ 13 / e-Vergabe platform practice).
- Minimum duty to extend the deadline after addenda below the threshold: not found in section 1.

## 3. At and after the deadline: opening, what bidders learn, the four evaluation stages

### Takeaway
Bidders attend an opening only where written bids are allowed in national tenders (§ 14a); with electronic-only bids (and in all EU procedures) the opening is internal and the bidder receives the list of bidders and final sums electronically without delay — this is the "Submissionsergebnis", from which the bidder reads its provisional rank. After that follow four internal stages: formal exclusion (§ 16), suitability (§ 16b), arithmetical/technical/economic check (§ 16c), and evaluation incl. price adequacy (§ 16d); the client is forbidden to tell bidders about the state of the procedure.

### Cited Findings
- § 14 VOB/A (electronic-only bids): opening by at least two representatives of the client "unverzüglich" after the deadline; no bidders. Abs. 6: "Bei Ausschreibungen stellt der Auftraggeber den Bietern die in Absatz 3 Buchstabe a bis d genannten Informationen unverzüglich elektronisch zur Verfügung" — names and addresses of bidders, final sums of the bids or lots, unconditional price reductions, number of Nebenangebote; bidders may inspect the Niederschrift and its supplements; the Niederschrift may not be published. — [dejure § 14 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/14.html)
- § 14a VOB/A (written bids allowed): Eröffnungstermin at which "nur die Bieter und ihre Bevollmächtigten" may be present; names, addresses, final sums and unconditional reductions are read out, and whether/by whom/how many Nebenangebote exist; nothing else. Bidders may sign the Niederschrift. Abs. 7: on request bidders are told without delay the names, the read-out and the recalculated final sums and the number of Nebenangebote. — [dejure § 14a VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/14a.html)
- VHB Richtlinien zu 313 Nr. 2.1: bidders are admitted only where written bids are allowed in section-1 tender procedures; "In allen anderen Vergabeverfahren findet der Öffnungstermin ohne Beteiligung von Bietern und/oder deren Bevollmächtigten statt." Nr. 3: the result of the opening is communicated only in Ausschreibungsverfahren (public/restricted tender, open/restricted procedure) — with an Eröffnungstermin on request, otherwise "unverzüglich nach Beendigung des Öffnungstermins an alle Bieter"; bidders get only the "Zusammenstellung der Angebote". Not communicated: content of bids and Nebenangebote, "den Stand des Vergabeverfahrens", the bids in the engere Wahl and the reasons. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 313](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- "Submissionsergebnis" is the market term for this record; only firms that submitted a bid can request it. (search snippet only) — [ibau Glossar](https://www.ibau.de/akademie/glossar/submissionsergebnis/)
- The recalculated final sums are entered into the Niederschrift after the check (§ 16c Abs. 3), so the list a bidder sees can change after the arithmetical check. — [dejure § 16c VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16c.html)
- Stage 1, § 16 Abs. 1 (mandatory exclusion): late bids; bids violating the form rules of § 13 Abs. 1 Nr. 1, 2, 5; bids without required documents where the client announced it will not request them; bids where requested declarations/proofs were not delivered within the calendar deadline; anti-competitive agreements; Nebenangebote or several Hauptangebote where not allowed; deliberately false statements on suitability. Abs. 2 (optional exclusion): insolvency, liquidation, grave misconduct, unpaid taxes/social contributions, not registered with the Berufsgenossenschaft. — [dejure § 16 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16.html)
- Stage 2, § 16b: in a public tender suitability is checked from the submitted proofs; the client may check the bids first and suitability afterwards "sofern sichergestellt ist, dass die anschließende Prüfung der Eignung unparteiisch und transparent erfolgt"; in restricted/negotiated awards only circumstances arising after the invitation count. — [dejure § 16b VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16b.html)
- Stage 3, § 16c: check "in rechnerischer, technischer und wirtschaftlicher Hinsicht". If total ≠ quantity × unit price, "so ist der Einheitspreis maßgebend"; for a Pauschalsumme the lump sum counts. — [dejure § 16c VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16c.html)
- Stage 4, § 16d: no award on a bid "mit einem unangemessen hohen oder niedrigen Preis"; only bids promising proper execution reach the "engere Wahl"; award to the "wirtschaftlichste Angebot"; only criteria published in the notice/documents count; unconditional discounts not listed at the designated place are ignored; unsolicited Skonti are not evaluated. — [dejure § 16d VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16d.html)
- Missing prices (VHB 321 Nr. 2.2): for insignificant items the client computes the rank with 0.00 EUR and with the highest price offered by others; if the rank changes the bid is excluded, otherwise the bidder is asked to add the price. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- A real state directly after "Submitted": "opened / Submissionsergebnis known" with the fields own sum, rank, distance to rank 1 and 2, number of bidders. For rank 1–3 the probability of follow-up requests (section 4) is high; for a low rank the tender is practically lost long before the formal rejection.
- Because the client must stay silent about the state of evaluation, "Submitted" on the board is a long silent stage whose only clock is the Bindefrist.

### Gaps
- Whether EU procedures (§ 14 EU) give bidders the same list of final sums was not read in the law text; the VHB guideline says the opening result is communicated in open and restricted procedures as well.
- No source found on how fast clients actually send the list in practice.

## 4. Events the bidder must react to after submission

### Takeaway
After submission the bidder can receive four kinds of dated requests, each with exclusion as the sanction: Nachforderung of missing documents (deadline set by the client, should not exceed 6 calendar days), Aufklärung of the bid content (reasonable deadline), Preisaufklärung for a suspiciously low price (in the federal VHB triggered at a 10 % gap; Formblätter 221/222 and 223 are requested, and the Urkalkulation may be inspected), and a request to extend the Bindefrist. Negotiation on price is forbidden under VOB/A tenders.

### Cited Findings
- § 16a VOB/A: the client must ask bidders who are in line for the award to submit, complete or correct missing/incomplete/faulty documents; "Es sind nur Unterlagen nachzufordern, die bereits mit dem Angebot vorzulegen waren"; "Fehlende Preisangaben dürfen nicht nachgefordert werden" (exception: insignificant items); the client may announce in the notice/documents that it will not request anything (Abs. 3); deadline "angemessen, nach dem Kalender bestimmt" and "Die Frist soll sechs Kalendertage nicht überschreiten" (Abs. 4); "Werden die nachgeforderten Unterlagen nicht innerhalb der Frist vorgelegt, ist das Angebot auszuschließen" (Abs. 5). — [dejure § 16a VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16a.html)
- § 16a EU VOB/A has the same structure and the same six-day soll-limit and exclusion; dejure marks it as re-worded by VOB/A 2026, in force 08.09.2026. — [dejure § 16a EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16a_EU.html)
- Since VOB/A 2019 the six days are no longer a rigid statutory period but a ceiling for the period the client sets; company-related documents may also be corrected. (search snippet only) — [Vergabeblog 2019](https://vergabeblog.de/2019-06-03/nachforderung-von-unterlagen-das-aendert-sich-durch-die-neue-vob-a-2019-ein-kurzer-ueberblick/)
- No second chance: documents already requested once and not delivered may not be requested again. (search snippet only) — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/de-de/expert-insights/vergabeverfahren-moeglichkeiten-grenzen-der-nachforderung)
- § 15 VOB/A: between opening and award the client may only ask for Aufklärung about suitability, the bid itself, Nebenangebote, the planned way of execution, origin of materials and "die Angemessenheit der Preise", if needed by inspecting the calculation; results are secret and recorded in text form; if the bidder refuses or lets a reasonable deadline pass "so ist sein Angebot auszuschließen" (Abs. 2); "Verhandlungen, besonders über Änderung der Angebote oder Preise, sind unstatthaft" (Abs. 3), except for unavoidable minor technical changes in Nebenangebote or functional tenders and the resulting price changes. — [dejure § 15 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/15.html)
- § 16d Abs. 1 Nr. 2 VOB/A: if a price appears unreasonably low and cannot be judged from the documents, the client must request in text form "Aufklärung über die Ermittlung der Preise für die Gesamtleistung" or for parts, and may set a reasonable deadline. — [dejure § 16d VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/16d.html)
- VHB trigger: doubts about low prices arise when the sums of one or a few bidders are considerably lower than the others or deviate considerably from the client's own estimate; "Solche Zweifel sind grundsätzlich bei einer Abweichung von zehn Prozent oder mehr anzunehmen." Then "zumindest die ausgefüllten Formblätter Preisermittlung 221 oder 222 und Aufgliederung der Einheitspreise 223" are to be requested. A low bid may be excluded only after text-form Aufklärung in which the bidder failed to prove a proper calculation. If only unreasonably high or low bids exist, the tender is to be cancelled. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321 Nr. 5.3](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Case law on the trigger: BGH 31.01.2017, X ZB 10/16 — competitors have a right that the client carries out the price check; whether the trigger lies at 20 % or already above 10 % was left open according to Rödl (gap in the case was 30 %), while Heuking reports the BGH as confirming 20 % to the next bid. (search snippets only; the two commentaries disagree) — [Rödl](https://www.roedl.com/insights/bgh-unternehmen-koennen-preispruefung-verlangen/); contradicted in part by [Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/de/news-events/newsletter-fachbeitraege/artikel/bgh-bieterschutz-bei-auffaellig-niedrigem-angebotspreis.html); [Vergabeblog](https://vergabeblog.de/2017-03-30/neues-zum-umgang-mit-dumping-angeboten-bgh-beschl-v-31-01-2017-x-zb-1016/)
- Formblätter 221–223: to be attached to the tender documents when the expected bid sum exceeds 50,000 EUR; may be dropped if the Urkalkulation is demanded instead. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien (Preisermittlung)](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Formblatt 223: above 50,000 EUR expected contract sum the client names the important price-determining items; above 100,000 EUR all items; "Die ausgefüllten Formblätter sind nur von den Bietern zu fordern, deren Angebote in der engeren Wahl sind"; data are confidential. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 223](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Formblatt 223 breaks each unit price into cost types (labour with time per unit, materials, equipment, other) incl. surcharges; it is built on 221 (Zuschlagskalkulation) or 222 (Endsummenkalkulation); it does not become part of the contract; not delivering it can lead to exclusion (BGH 07.06.2005, X ZR 19/02 cited). — [bauprofessor, Aufgliederung Einheitspreise 223](https://www.bauprofessor.de/aufgliederung-einheitspreise-efb-223/)
- Aufklärung in the VHB: discussions with bidders about the data in Formblätter 221/222/223 count as Aufklärung; "Bei Zweifeln an deren Schlüssigkeit oder Richtigkeit soll die Vergabestelle Klärung durch Einsichtnahme in die Urkalkulation herbeiführen und nötigenfalls die Berichtigung in den Formblättern verlangen"; if non-delivery or refusal hinders a proper evaluation "ist das Angebot unberücksichtigt zu lassen". — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321 Nr. 6](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Urkalkulation on request: Formblatt 216 lists under documents on request "Urkalkulation (die Urkalkulation wird für die Prüfung der Preise geöffnet, im Anschluss wieder verschlossen)". — [VHB Bund, Formblatt 216](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Bindefristverlängerung: the VHB tells the Vergabestelle to seek, in time before the Bindefrist ends, "eine einheitliche Fristverlängerung (in Textform)" from the bidders (in the context of review proceedings). — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 100 Nr. 6.3.5](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Consequence of refusing or not answering: the bid's binding effect lapses and the bid drops out; a later unilateral consent does not revive it (Vergabekammer decision); OLG Celle 30.01.2020, 13 Verg 14/19 concerns exclusion for not confirming the requested extension, the bid lapsing under the BGB rules on offers; BayObLG 21.11.2025, Verg 11/25 e: repeated or disproportionately long extensions without a clear prospect of award burden bidders unreasonably; a tender clause demanding advance consent to an extension in case of review proceedings was accepted by a Vergabekammer. (search snippets only) — [OLG Celle via Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/fileadmin/Aktuelles/OLG_Celle_30.01.20_13_Verg_14-19_1086.pdf); [BayObLG Verg 11/25 e](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2025-N-41341?hl=true); [cosinex](https://blog.cosinex.de/2024/03/12/vergaberecht-bindefrist-verlaengerung/)
- Consenting does not waive money: a plain extension leaves the offer unchanged including its execution dates (BGH VII ZR 213/08); "eine einvernehmliche Bindefristverlängerung schließt die Geltendmachung verzögerungsbedingter Ansprüche auf zusätzliche Vergütung nicht aus". (search snippets only) — [IWW](https://www.iww.de/pbp/archiv/voba-vergaben-bgh-erleichtert-vergabe-und-abrechnung-von-auftraegen-bei-terminverzoegerungen-f15804); [rewis BGH VII ZR 213/08](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/pbk-22-07-2010-vii-zr-21308/)

### Inferences
- Missing states between "Submitted" and "Award", each with a due date and "exclusion" as the consequence: "Nachforderung open (due ≤ 6 KT)", "Aufklärung requested / Aufklärungsgespräch scheduled", "Preisaufklärung: Formblatt 221/222/223 or Urkalkulation requested", "Bindefristverlängerung requested (decide: consent / consent with reservation / refuse = leave the tender)".
- A request for Formblatt 223 is itself a signal: by the VHB rule it goes only to bids in the engere Wahl.
- Formblatt 223 is exactly the split CLAUDE.md describes (labour + material + equipment + other, plus overheads); a tool holding the calculation could fill it. This is an inference from the form's content, not something a source states.
- A bidder more than 10 % below the next bid or the client's estimate should expect a Preisaufklärung at federal clients; the bidder sees this gap from the Submissionsergebnis the same day.

### Gaps
- Frequency: no statistics found on how often § 16a requests, Aufklärungen, Preisaufklärungen or Bindefrist extensions occur. Sources describe them as routine but give no numbers.
- No source read on the usual format of an Aufklärungsgespräch (who attends, minutes) beyond "results in text form, secret".
- The VHB text read is "Stand 2019/2023"; whether the 10 % rule and Formblatt numbers are unchanged in a current edition was not confirmed.

## 5. The end: Bindefrist, information of unsuccessful bidders, Zuschlag, late award, Aufhebung, remedies

### Takeaway
The bid is binding from the end of the bid period for normally 30 calendar days below the threshold and 60 above. Above the threshold every losing bidder receives a pre-award notice (name of the winner, reasons, earliest contract date) and the contract may not be concluded for 10 (electronic) or 15 days — this is the only window for a review application. Below the threshold there is, under VOB/A alone, no advance notice: losers are told when the award is made, and reasons only on request within 15 days. Three further end states exist besides won/lost: tender cancelled (Aufhebung), excluded, and binding period lapsed.

### Cited Findings
- Bindefrist below the threshold (§ 10 Abs. 4–6 VOB/A): as short as possible; more than 30 KT only in justified cases; end stated as a calendar date; starts when the Angebotsfrist ends; also applies to Freihändige Vergabe. — [dejure § 10 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10.html)
- Bindefrist above the threshold: "Die Bindefrist beträgt regelmäßig 60 Kalendertage"; longer in justified cases. — [dejure § 10a EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/10a_EU.html)
- VHB: standard 30 KT (section 1) / 60 KT (section 2) / 30 KT (section 3); longer periods must be justified in the Vergabevermerk. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 111 Nr. 6.3](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Zuschlag (§ 18 VOB/A): "möglichst bald, mindestens aber so rechtzeitig zu erteilen" that the bidder receives it before the Bindefrist ends; if the award comes with extensions, restrictions or changes, or late, the bidder "ist ... aufzufordern, sich unverzüglich über die Annahme zu erklären" (i.e. it is a new offer the bidder may refuse). — [dejure § 18 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/18.html)
- Information below the threshold (§ 19 VOB/A): excluded bidders and those not in the engere Wahl are to be informed "unverzüglich", the remaining bidders once the award is made (Abs. 1); on a text-form request the reasons, plus the features and advantages of the winning bid and the winner's name, within 15 KT (Abs. 2); drafts, samples etc. are returned if requested in the bid or within 30 KT after rejection (Abs. 4). — [dejure § 19 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/19.html)
- Information above the threshold (§ 134 GWB, version since 01.07.2026; mirrored in § 19 EU VOB/A): text-form notice to the bidders not considered with the name of the winner, the reasons and the earliest date of contract conclusion; contract only 15 KT after sending, 10 KT if sent electronically or by fax; the period starts the day after sending regardless of receipt; exceptions: urgent negotiated procedure without competition, call-offs under framework agreements. On request further details within 15 KT (§ 19 EU Abs. 4). — [dejure § 134 GWB](https://dejure.org/gesetze/GWB/134.html); [dejure § 19 EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/19_EU.html)
- VHB forms for these letters: 332 "Absageschreiben nach § 19 VOB/A", 334 "Informations-, Absageschreiben nach § 134 GWB", 338 "Auftragsschreiben" (339 Beiblatt). — [VHB Bund, Formblattverzeichnis](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Aufhebung (§ 17 VOB/A): possible if "kein Angebot eingegangen ist, das den Ausschreibungsbedingungen entspricht", "die Vergabeunterlagen grundlegend geändert werden müssen", or "andere schwerwiegende Gründe bestehen"; bidders are informed without delay in text form with reasons and, where applicable, the intention to start a new procedure. — [dejure § 17 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/17.html)
- VHB: if only bids with unexpectedly high prices exist and the client's estimate is confirmed, the tender can be cancelled; if only unreasonably high/low bids exist it is to be cancelled. — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321 Nr. 5.3/5.4](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Late award and its price/date consequences (BGH line 2009–2012): a later award after extended Bindefristen does not by itself create a claim (VII ZR 82/08); the award is read as keeping the tendered dates; if the construction period thereby shifts, additional pay follows analogous to § 2 Nr. 5 VOB/B, limited to costs caused by the shift (VII ZR 202/09); if the award letter itself sets new dates it is a modified acceptance — the bidder who accepts performs at the agreed prices (VII ZR 193/10). (search snippets only; full judgments not read) — [Rechtslupe](https://www.rechtslupe.de/wirtschaftsrecht/mehrverguetung-nach-einem-verzoegerten-vergabeverfahren-39127); [rewis BGH VII ZR 202/09](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/qah-08-03-2012-vii-zr-20209/); [rewis BGH VII ZR 193/10](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/onw-06-09-2012-vii-zr-19310/)
- Review above the threshold: the VK acts only on application; standing needs interest in the contract and a claimed violation with (threatened) damage (§ 160 Abs. 1–2 GWB). — [dejure § 160 GWB](https://dejure.org/gesetze/GWB/160.html)
- After delivery of the review application the client may not award (Suspensiveffekt). — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 100 Nr. 6.3.2](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Changed 01.07.2026 (Vergabebeschleunigungsgesetz): the VK decides within five weeks, extension at most two weeks (§ 167); if the client wins before the VK the award ban ends with the decision (§ 169 Abs. 1 n.F.) and the sofortige Beschwerde no longer has suspensive effect (§ 173 Abs. 1 n.F.) — the losing bidder is then mostly left with damages; OLG Düsseldorf referred the parallel rule in the BwBBG to the Federal Constitutional Court (18.05.2026, Verg 6/26); proceedings in writing or electronically, video hearings, electronic file access. Procedures started before 01.07.2026 run under the old law (§ 187 Abs. 2 GWB n.F.). — [GvW Graf von Westphalen](https://www.gvw.com/aktuelles/blog/detail/vergaberechtsnovelle-2026-inkrafttreten-des-vergabebeschleunigungsgesetzes)
- Below the threshold: no notice duty comparable to § 134 GWB, so bidders often learn of the award too late; primary protection is an einstweilige Verfügung at the civil court, which must be issued and served before the award; § 21 VOB/A names a Nachprüfungsstelle (supervisory authority / VOB-Stelle). (search snippets only) — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/de-de/expert-insights/rechtsschutzmoeglichkeiten-unterhalb-der-eu-schwellenwerte); [Bayernportal VOB-Stelle](https://www.bayernportal.de/dokumente/leistung/292069283902)
- Recent example: LG Aurich 12.02.2026, 2 O 98/26 — injunction for an excluded lowest bidder (429,798.08 EUR, dredging works under VOB/A) based on culpa in contrahendo; Niedersachsen has an information and waiting duty in § 16 NTVergG but no review body, hence the civil court. — [Vergabeblog](https://vergabeblog.de/2026-07-27/effektiver-eilrechtsschutz-bei-bauvergaben-im-unterschwellenbereich/)
- Some Länder require a pre-award notice with a waiting period below the threshold: Thüringen (§ 19 ThürVgG), Sachsen-Anhalt, Sachsen (§ 8 SächsVG), Rheinland-Pfalz. (search snippets only; day counts not verified) — [Wolters Kluwer, Vorabinformation](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/de-de/expert-insights/vorabinformationsschreiben-vergabeverfahren)

### Inferences
- End states the board lacks: "lost (Absage received; reasons requested / received)", "pre-award notice received – standstill running until [date]" (both for the winner and the loser), "Rüge / Nachprüfungsverfahren pending", "tender cancelled (Aufhebung; possibly re-tender → new card linked to the old one)", "excluded (with ground)", "Bindefrist expired without award", "award with changes – acceptance pending".
- For a lost bid the useful record is: winner's name, winning sum (from Submissionsergebnis), own rank and gap, reasons from the § 19 Abs. 2 answer. The request for reasons is something the bidder must actively send; it is not automatic below the threshold.
- The Bindefrist end date is the one hard date of the "Submitted" stage: the estimator's supplier and subcontractor quotes must stay valid until then, and each extension request reopens that question.

### Gaps
- Day counts of the Länder pre-award waiting periods were not verified.
- § 21 VOB/A text was not read.
- No data on how many tenders end in Aufhebung or how long evaluation takes in practice.
- BGH judgments on late awards were only seen as snippets.

## 6. After the award: award letter, securities, calculation documents, start of works, where Nachträge begin

### Takeaway
The contract comes into being with the Zuschlag (Auftragsschreiben) on the unchanged bid; what follows is a short list of dated obligations — securities (normally none below 250,000 EUR contract sum), confirmation documents, handing over the Urkalkulation in a sealed envelope — and then the start of works. The Urkalkulation (or since 2018 the actual costs) is the price base for Nachträge under § 2 VOB/B.

### Cited Findings
- Award = acceptance within the Bindefrist; only an award with changes or after the deadline needs the bidder's declaration of acceptance. — [dejure § 18 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/18.html)
- Securities: below 250,000 EUR contract sum (net) security for performance is to be waived and, as a rule, also security for defects (§ 9c VOB/A); performance security towards public clients should not exceed 5 % of the contract sum. (search snippets only) — [lxgesetze § 9c VOB/A](https://lxgesetze.de/vob-a-2019/9c); [bauprofessor Vertragserfüllungssicherheit](https://www.bauprofessor.de/vertragserfuellungssicherheit/)
- The Bürgschaft is to be handed in within 18 Werktage after contract conclusion (§ 17 Abs. 7 VOB/B), otherwise the client withholds from payments. (search snippet only) — [ADD Rheinland-Pfalz Arbeitsblatt Nr. 20](https://wayback.edoweb-rlp.de/weltweit/20241029mp_/https:/add.rlp.de/fileadmin/add/Abteilung_4/Vergaberecht/ARBEITSBLATT_Nr.20_2022-09.pdf)
- VHB contract terms (Besondere Vertragsbedingungen form) set the start of execution as "spätestens ... Werktage nach Zugang des Auftragsschreibens" and express securities and Vertragsstrafe as a percentage "der im Auftragsschreiben genannten Auftragssumme". — [VHB Bund, Formblatt 214](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Urkalkulation: documents the calculation behind the bid; not named in the VOB; handed over "mit dem Angebot oder spätestens zum Vertragsabschluss", usually "in einem verschlossenen Umschlag"; does not become part of the contract; the parties should fix whether it may be opened only jointly or by the client for checking Nachträge; returned after the unconditional final payment. — [bauprofessor Urkalkulation](https://www.bauprofessor.de/urkalkulation/)
- Role for Nachträge: OLG Düsseldorf 21.11.2014, 22 U 37/14 — a claim under § 2 Nr. 5/6 VOB/B without traceable price bases is to be dismissed; under the BGB the contractor may rely on "die Ansätze in einer vereinbarungsgemäß hinterlegten Urkalkulation" (§ 650c Abs. 2 BGB). — [bauprofessor Urkalkulation](https://www.bauprofessor.de/urkalkulation/)
- The VHB itself notes the shift that Nachtrag pay is "nicht mehr anhand der Urkalkulation fortgeschrieben" (context: newer case law on actual costs). — [VHB Bund Lesefassung, introductory notes](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)
- Formblätter 221–223 do not become part of the contract "weil im Vertrag nur die Preise, nicht aber die Art ihres Zustandekommens ... vereinbart werden". — [VHB Bund, Richtlinien zu 321 Nr. 5.1.3](https://fib-bund.de/Inhalt/Vergabe/VHB/VHB_2017_Lesefassung_2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- States after "Award": "Auftragsschreiben received", "securities due (date)", "Urkalkulation handed over", "Auftragskalkulation / Arbeitskalkulation prepared", "Baubeginn". After Baubeginn the object changes from bid to contract and Nachträge (§ 2 Abs. 3, 5, 6 VOB/B) start; findings from the LV check (quantity risk, missing items) are the raw material for them.

### Gaps
- § 5 Abs. 2 VOB/B (start within 12 Werktage after the client's call if no date is agreed) and § 2 VOB/B were not read in the law text; treat the 12-Werktage figure as unverified.
- The step from Angebotskalkulation to Auftragskalkulation/Arbeitskalkulation was not sourced.
- Exact wording of Formblatt 338 (Auftragsschreiben) not extracted.

## 7. Private clients (no VOB/A): what replaces the formal steps

### Takeaway
A private client is not bound by VOB/A, so the whole formal chain (public opening, ban on negotiation, Nachforderung rules, standstill) is absent; its place is taken by one or more Vergabeverhandlungen / Bietergespräche in which scope, dates, terms and above all price are negotiated, recorded in a signed Verhandlungsprotokoll that becomes part of the contract. Under VOB/A the same negotiation is forbidden in tender procedures (§ 15 Abs. 3) and allowed only in the Verhandlungsverfahren above the threshold.

### Cited Findings
- Confirmation of the ban: "Verhandlungen, besonders über Änderung der Angebote oder Preise, sind unstatthaft" (§ 15 Abs. 3 VOB/A), with the narrow exception for Nebenangebote / functional tenders. — [dejure § 15 VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/15.html)
- Contrast inside public law: in the Verhandlungsverfahren Erst- and Folgeangebote are negotiated to improve them, minimum requirements and award criteria excluded, ending with a common deadline for final bids. — [dejure § 3b EU VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/3b_EU.html)
- Private clients are defined as all non-public clients: "Immobilieninvestoren, Bauträger, Industrieunternehmen, Privatpersonen". (search snippet only) — [Hochschule Augsburg, Skript AVA](https://moodle.hs-augsburg.de/pluginfile.php/616870/mod_resource/content/0/Skript%20AVA%2024-2.pdf)
- Model protocol of the DVP (Deutscher Verband für Projektmanagement in der Bau- und Immobilienwirtschaft), "J. Verhandlungsprotokoll (Gewerkvergabe)", 2019 — its fields show what a private award negotiation covers:
  - 0: the bidder's representative declares full authority, or limited authority with approval by management to follow by a date; "aus dem Führen dieser Verhandlungen [resultieren] keine Ansprüche auf Auftragserteilung oder Schadenersatzansprüche".
  - 1: subject = the bid of [date], with its sum "ungeprüft" and "rechnerisch und sachlich geprüft".
  - 2–3: subcontractors; whether the scope is complete and free of contradictions; Bedenken of the contractor; shop drawings; staff; changes to the scope since the tender (new plan index) — the bidder states whether the effect can be priced today or "im finalen Angebot".
  - 4 Vergütung: "Angebotssumme / Änderungen gem. Ziff. 3.5 / eingeräumter Nachlass % / neue Angebotssumme = Auftragssumme netto"; choice between Einheitspreisvertrag (unit prices fixed for the construction period) and "Pauschalpreis (Pauschalpreisvertrag)"; tick box "Der vereinbarte Nachlass gilt auch für Nachträge."
  - 5: payment plan, Skonto in % on interim and final invoices, securities.
  - 6: Baubeginn, intermediate dates, completion; tick box that a shift of the start by the client by up to X has no effect on periods or price.
  - 7–13: certificates (Handelsregister, Berufsgenossenschaft, § 48b EStG), Vertragsstrafe, liability insurance, site manager, cost sharing (site power, water, cleaning, Bauwesenversicherung), hourly rates.
  - 14: "Der Bieter hält sich an sein Angebot gemäß diesem Verhandlungsprotokoll gebunden bis zum ... / bis zu sechs Wochen nach Verhandlung"; if a final offer is requested: "Das finale Angebot ist spätestens zum ... beim AG einzureichen und der AN hält sich dann hieran ... bis zu sechs Wochen nach Einreichung gebunden."
  - Note for users: the protocol alone is not meant to be the contract; an order should follow in a contract that refers to it (VOB/B etc.).
  — [DVP Vertragsmuster J, Verhandlungsprotokoll](https://dvpev.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/DVP_Vertragsmuster_online_J-Verhandlungsprotokoll_080519.pdf)
- A negotiation protocol should be drawn up when negotiations follow the bid and be listed as a contract component; a corporate template (Telekom AVB Bau) ranks the Verhandlungsprotokoll directly after the contract itself. (search snippets only) — [Haufe, Bauvertrag Anmerkungen](https://haufe.de/id/beitrag/14-bauvertrag-ii-anmerkungen-HI16114390.html); [Telekom AVB Bau](https://www.Telekom.com/resource/blob/532588/9156d7004d32db66bcee67984ccccdc1/dl-avb-bau-dsgvo-mai2018-data.pdf)
- Whether a Nachlass also applies to later Nachträge is a recurring dispute. (search snippet only; conclusion of the article not read) — [bauprofessor](https://www.bauprofessor.de/news/gelten-preisnachlaesse-auch-fuer-vertragsnachtraege/)
- Subcontracting: the Hauptverband der Deutschen Bauindustrie publishes a model Nachunternehmervertrag (2024). (search result only, not read) — [Bauindustrie NU-Vertrag 2024](https://www.bauindustrie.de/fileadmin/bauindustrie.de/Themen/Wirtschaft_Recht/fsb_2024-3_nachunternehmervertrag_inland.pdf)

### Inferences
- For private tenders the stage between "Submitted" and "Award" is not silent but active: "invited to Vergabegespräch", "negotiation round n", "final offer (letztes / finales Angebot) requested by [date]", "bound to negotiated result until [date]", "contract draft in review", "awarded / lost (often without reasons)". There is no opening with published sums, so the rank is unknown unless the client says so.
- The negotiated variables are visible in the DVP form: Nachlass in %, Pauschalierung (unit-price contract turned into a lump sum, which moves the quantity risk to the contractor), Skonto, shifted dates without compensation, cost-sharing deductions, Nachlass also on Nachträge. A what-if price tool matches this stage directly.
- The same pattern applies one level down: the main contractor negotiates with its own subcontractors and suppliers in exactly this way (NU-Vertrag) while bound to the client by its bid.

### Gaps
- No good German source found that describes the typical number of rounds, the "letztes Angebot" custom or how often private clients re-open price after the last offer; the search mainly returned public-law and Austrian material. The description above rests on one model form (DVP) and general contract commentary.
- GU-Vertrag specifics (GMP, Pauschalfestpreis, open-book) were not researched.
- The BGB rules behind it (§§ 145–150 BGB: binding period of an offer, modified acceptance as new offer) were not read in the law text.

### Inferences
- Minimal extension of the five-stage board that covers most of this without new columns: keep the five stages, add (a) a pre-stage "Application" for procedures with Teilnahmewettbewerb, (b) inside "Submitted" a sub-status with a due date (opened/rank, Nachforderung, Aufklärung, Preisaufklärung, Bindefrist extension, standstill, review), and (c) a typed outcome instead of only "Award": awarded, lost, excluded, cancelled, withdrawn, expired — each with the date and the document that proves it.
- The dates worth storing per tender: Teilnahmefrist, cut-off for questions, Angebotsfrist/Öffnungstermin, Bindefrist end (and each extension), due date of each request, date the § 134 notice was sent (+10/15), Baubeginn.
- The procedure type decides which states can occur (Teilnahmeantrag only with competition; standstill only above the threshold or in certain Länder; negotiation rounds only in Verhandlungsverfahren / Freihändige Vergabe / private), so procedure type and client type (public below threshold, public above, private) should be attributes of the tender.

### Gaps

- UVgO (supplies/services below threshold) is not relevant for construction and was not covered.

## 9. What changed 2024–2026 (to be noted when citing "VOB/A 2019")

### Takeaway
Section 1 of VOB/A (below threshold) is still the 2019 edition, but with new value limits since 01.01.2026; sections 2 and 3 (EU, defence) were re-issued as "VOB/A 2026" and are binding since 08.09.2026; the GWB/VgV were changed by the Vergabebeschleunigungsgesetz with effect from 01.07.2026. The bidder-side deadlines researched here (10/15-day standstill, Rüge periods, 6-day Nachforderung ceiling, 35/30-day bid periods, 60-day Bindefrist) appear unchanged in the versions read.

### Cited Findings
- EU threshold for works 5,404,000 EUR for 2026–2027 (down from 5,538,000). - § 3a VOB/A value limits from 01.01.2026: 150,000 / 100,000 / 50,000 EUR (restricted without competition / Freihändige Vergabe / Direktauftrag). — [dejure § 3a VOB/A](https://dejure.org/gesetze/VOB-A/3a.html); [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/vergabe-am-bau-2026-gelten-deutlich-hoehere-wertgrenzen)

### Inferences
- "VOB/A 2019" is now only correct as a label for section 1; for EU-wide tenders started after 08.09.2026 cite "VOB/A 2026 (Abschnitt 2)". Paragraph numbers used above for the EU section are as dejure shows them now.
- The shift to Eigenerklärungen and "bid check first" above the threshold moves document requests from the bid date to the phase after opening — i.e. more post-submission requests to the best-placed bidders, not fewer. This is an inference from the rule change, not a measured effect.

- Whether the BMWSB has issued its decree applying VOB/A 2026 for federal construction, and whether the VHB was re-issued, was not found.
- Whether section 1 will be aligned with the 2026 text is unknown.
