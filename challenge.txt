Challenge A: AI-Powered Cost Estimation and BoQ Analysis

Working Title
"AI Estimation and Bidding Assistant for Bills of Quantities"

Problem Statement
Bills of quantities (BoQs) often contain thousands of line items, multiple cost groups and cross-trade dependencies. Companies lack the time and staff to review them in detail, identify risks and learn from past projects. Typos, calculation errors and overlooked change order potential can have major financial consequences.

Challenge Objective
Build a prototype for intelligent BoQ analysis:
Import and parse long BoQs (PDF, GAEB, Excel)
Error detection (priority): missing items, unclear descriptions, contradictions, typos
Risk assessment: flag imprecise scope descriptions and unusual quantities
Detect change order potential and likely disputes during execution
Optional: suggest unit prices or markups from historical project data

Milestones
BoQ parsing and structured data extraction
Error detection and plausibility checks
Risk scoring and change order detection
Integration of historical project data (optional)
What-if analyses (material prices, alternatives)

Data
Anonymized sample BoQs from various trades and project sizes (GAEB preferred)
Optional: historical project data (planned vs. actual costs, change order summaries)
Real-world examples of faulty BoQ items
Unit price catalogs or benchmarks (if available)

Deliverables
Demo of a web app or assistant tool with upload and risk report
Pitch from an estimator's perspective: How does the prototype change the bidding process?
