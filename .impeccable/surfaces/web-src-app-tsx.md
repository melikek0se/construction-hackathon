# Surface brief: LV analysis workspace (`web/src/App.tsx`)

Scope: the single screen of the web app. Mode: Operate. Viewed on a projector by a jury and on a laptop by an
estimator. Task: see where in the LV the trouble sits, open a flagged item, read evidence, reason, action and
the German LV text. Constraints: LV text stays German; no invented money figures; GAEB only.
Memorable moment: the two pipe sizes of a contradictory item drawn side by side, to scale.
Unresolved: report export; prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The LV is a site under construction, read as a strip map; trouble is a numbered sign-coloured block you
can read from the back of the room. Refuses the dashboard of stat tiles over a table.

OWN-WORLD: German site safety signage. Signal yellow band, black DIN-like condensed capitals, white sign
plates with a black keyline, concrete greys; red circle / yellow triangle / blue square carry severity.

STORY: Load an LV, see which section holds the columns, click one, read the evidence and the action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: yellow band with a hazard stripe; left two thirds the LV map (one named row per section, one
mark per item, flagged items as numbered blocks); right third the findings by item, replaced by the item sheet
(picture of the item, evidence, action) on selection. The 3D plan of the first build was dropped by the user.

FORM: site safety signage system, candidate 6 of 7; seed key ae746eef. Signature interaction: click a block,
see the item drawn. Raises — airport wayfinding: item numbers at monumental scale;
star atlas: severity on one fixed height ramp; split-flap: the plan never re-flows, only columns change state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
